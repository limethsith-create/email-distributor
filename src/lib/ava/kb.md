# Ava's guide to Aviance and the hub

Ava reads this through the `search_kb` tool. Each `##` section is one answer. Keep it free of real
people's names, email addresses and phone numbers.

## The whole process (how a trial works, start to finish)
Every client goes the same way: they apply on the website → the owner reads it and says yes (or no) →
they book and have the onboarding call → their domain and two inboxes are bought and set up →
the inboxes warm up for about two weeks → near the end of warm-up there is the launch call, where the owner
goes through their list and their emails with them → sending runs for 30 days (a paying client keeps sending)
→ replies are sorted and the reply bot answers simple questions → prospects book calls into the client's calendar
→ on day 30 the trial client gets a decision page → if they pick a plan the month-one invoice goes out and the owner
marks it paid. The hub shows this as five steps: 1 Applied, 2 Onboarding call, 3 Setting up, 4 Sending emails, 5 Done.

## Applying (new applications)
Someone applies on the website's trial form, or asks for a paid plan (Starter, Growth or Scale). They appear at the top
of Trials (or Paying clients for a paid plan) under New applications, with a match percent from the fit check.
Application (Word) downloads everything they wrote plus "What we found": a short brief about the company with where
each fact comes from. Research again looks the company up again.

## Your yes (say yes / say no)
Say yes emails them at once, asking them to book the onboarding call — from then on the trial runs by itself and
moves to step 2. If three trials are already running they join the waiting list and start when a place opens.
Say no asks for a short reason first, then sends a polite no; they move to Done, not taken. Nothing is sent until
the owner confirms. Only the owner can say yes or no.

## The onboarding call
After the yes they get one email asking them to book the onboarding call, and reminders if they don't book.
When they pick a time it waits in the Calendar for the owner's yes. The call card on the client page has
Mark call booked, Call done, They didn't show, Send the email again and Stop reminders. On the call the owner
learns what they sell, who they sell to and what a good call looks like for them.

## Setting up inboxes (step 3: domain and inboxes)
Setting up means buying the client's own sending domain (a look-alike of their main one) and two inboxes.
With CheapInboxes connected (Settings › Inboxes & domains) the big button shows exactly what to buy; the owner buys
it in his CheapInboxes account and the system finds the purchase and connects everything by itself.
Without it, Buy & paste lets the owner paste the domain and the inbox logins. The machine then checks the domain's
DNS (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) before warm-up.

## Warm-up
Warming up means the new inboxes send and answer friendly emails with other accounts (the warm-up circle) for about
two weeks, so email providers learn to trust them and our emails land in the inbox, not spam. The client's Warm-up
card shows "day N of about 14" and how many reach the inbox (the inbox rate). An inbox is ready at 90% on two days in
a row. The circle needs at least 8 members: when it is short the owner adds warm-up helpers (free Gmail, Yahoo, AOL,
iCloud, GMX, WEB.DE or Yandex accounts) in Settings › Warm-up with Add a helper → Test and add.

## The launch call
Near the end of warm-up the client gets the launch-call invite. On the call the owner goes through their lead list
and their emails with them (sharing the approval page). He presses Approved on the call when they say OK — sending
starts on Day 1. If they already approved on the page, the call can be skipped (Skip the call).

## Sending (days 1 to 30)
Sending runs for 30 days from Day 1 for a trial. Each inbox starts slowly and sends a few more each day (at most 25
cold emails per inbox per day). The list shows "Day 12 of 30". Only one email per person — no follow-ups unless
switched on. If too many bounce (about 2–3%), sending slows down or pauses by itself and the owner gets an alert.
Opens are not tracked for clients on purpose (trackers hurt landing in the inbox), so Opened is blank for them.

## Replies and the reply bot
Every reply is read and sorted: interested, question, not now, wrong person, no, unsubscribe, out of office and so on.
Interested people are passed to the client as hot leads. A STOP or a no is never emailed again, for any client.
The reply bot answers simple questions with fixed answers — the booking link and free times, who we are, how we got
their address, "check back later" — it never makes things up. Switch it off for one person with the switch under
Messages on their page; Settings › Reply bot lists every rule.

## Calls (booked calls, qualified calls, disputes)
Calls booked from our emails land in the client's calendar. A call is qualified when it was held, with the right kind
of person, booked from our outreach. No-shows get a rebook email. If the client says a call doesn't count it shows as
a dispute for the owner to decide (uphold or overturn).

## The day-30 decision
On day 30 a trial client gets the decision page with their numbers, one plan we recommend and a 24-hour bonus.
They can pick a plan (Start), ask to talk, or say not now. With no qualified call yet, the trial keeps sending free
until the first one, up to 60 sending days (an extension). Paying clients have no day 30.

## The invoice (money)
When a trial client picks a plan, or a paying client signs the plan agreement, the month-one invoice goes out.
When the money lands the owner presses Mark paid on the to-do. Plans: Starter, Growth and Scale.
Money is only for the owner — team members never see amounts.

## Trials page
Lists every trial client. Needs you is at the top in red with what to do; In progress are the ones running with
nothing needed; Done, not taken is folded at the bottom. The stage tiles (Applied, Booking the call, Call booked,
Setting up, Warming up, Sending, Finished) count clients at each stage — tap one to filter. At the bottom:
Add a trial client yourself (or press N). Tap a row to open the client.

## Paying clients page
Works like Trials but for clients on Starter, Growth or Scale: new paid-plan applications, the stage tiles and
every paying client. The owner also sees money received at the top. Add a paying client yourself is at the bottom.
Plan call requests (inquiries: people who booked a call about a paid plan from the website) are at the bottom too.

## Calendar page
Every call in Sri Lanka time with US Eastern beside it: onboarding calls, launch calls, meetings the owner added and
busy blocks. A time a client asked for waits at the top: Say yes (sends the time and an invite), Suggest another
time, or Say no. A confirmed call has a Join Google Meet button when Google Meet is connected.

## Team page
Everyone who uses the hub: who is in the hub now, where they are, what they are working on (their status line —
write yours under "What are you working on"), and which clients each person looks after. The owner picks each
person's clients with Choose clients. Team members can see everything but change nothing, and never see money.

## My stats and Saved history
My stats is the owner's own outreach (Aviance's own cold emails): emails sent, opened, replies, bounced, emails per
day, by inbox, and every reply. Saved history keeps full copies as spreadsheets. Save and start fresh saves a copy,
then clears the numbers so My stats starts from zero — people already emailed are never emailed again.

## Activity page
The owner's page: who signed in and out, for how long, what they opened, and new accounts waiting for approval.
Approve lets a person in as a team member (read-only).

## Settings sections
Settings has named sections, each saying its state in one word: Alerts (every message from the system, Mark as seen),
Phone alerts (push to the phone; on iPhone add the hub to the Home Screen first), Your details (name, address, the
onboarding inbox, call link, PayPal, Wise, Clutch), Keys (one card per service key: paste, Test and save; a saved key
is never shown again — this is also where Ava's AI keys go), Google Meet (connect Google so every confirmed call gets a
Meet link), Inboxes & domains (CheapInboxes), Warm-up (helpers), Reply bot (its rules), Test run, Is everything
running? (last check-in, last email sent, alerts), Behind the scenes (every to-do, all trials by stage, the waiting
list), Advanced (Mission Control), Light or dark, and Your account (log out). Settings is for the owner.

## The client page
One card per client: the five steps (Applied, Onboarding call, Setting up, Sending emails, Done), a one-line status,
and — when something needs the owner — one big button for the most important thing. Under it, Open their email
system shows everything else. Messages (the owner's emails with the client, the reply box, the reply-bot switch)
are on the client page too.

## The email system: Shared and Only you tabs
A client's email system has a switch with two sides. Shared with the client — exactly what the client sees on
their own page: Overview (emails sent, opened, replies, bounced, interested, calls booked, emails per day),
Conversations (every thread with a prospect), Emails sent, Calls and Messages. Never money or internal notes.
Only you (the owner and the team, never the client): Money & plan (the owner only), Health (inboxes, warm-up,
landing in the inbox, growth), Leads & emails, and Setup & history (who can see their page, the application, calls,
disputes, parts, history, actions).

## Give access (the client's own page)
Open the client → their email system → Setup. Under Who can see this, type an email address from their business and
press Give access: they get a private link to their own live page (read-only). Stop all access turns every old link
off. Only the owner can give access; Ava can open the place but never sends the email herself.

## Test run
Settings › Test run loads example clients (a finished trial, a paying client and a trial mid-way) marked with a Test
tag, so the owner can click through a whole month. No real email is ever sent for them and nothing can be changed on
them. Remove the test run takes them away; real clients aren't touched. Owner only.

## Alerts, the bell and search
The bell at the top lists what needs you right now. Settings › Alerts has every message, not seen first, with Mark as
seen. Press Ctrl/Cmd K (or /) to find any client or page; Ctrl/Cmd J talks to Ava.

## Roles (owner and team members)
The owner can do everything. A team member sees the same screens but changes nothing (the buttons are hidden), and
money is only for the owner. New team members make an account on the sign-in page and the owner approves them in
Activity. Everyone can set their own status line and ask Ava to note a change request.

## Ava and privacy
Ava answers from the hub's data through the machine. The AI services she uses do not train on what is sent
(Groq, Cerebras, and Gemini or OpenRouter only on paid, no-training settings). Ava's tools never send prospects'
names, email addresses, phone numbers or message text — only company names, counts, stages, dates and rates.
What you type or say to her is sent as you wrote it, so avoid personal details. She can't change anything herself:
she proposes a button and you press it. She can't edit the system's code — she writes it down as a change request.

## FAQ: Why is Opened blank for a client?
Client emails go out without an open tracker (trackers hurt landing in the inbox), so there is nothing to count.
Only the owner's own outreach on My stats tracks opens.

## FAQ: What is a bounce?
An email that came back because the address doesn't exist or the mailbox refused it. A few are normal; about 2–3%
slows or pauses sending by itself to protect the inboxes.

## FAQ: How many trials can run at once?
Three. Anyone else the owner says yes to joins the waiting list and starts when a place opens.

## FAQ: How long until sending starts?
Roughly: onboarding call within a few days of the yes, a day or two to buy and set up the inboxes, about 14 days of
warm-up, then the launch call and Day 1. So about three weeks from the yes.

## FAQ: What does the client see?
Only their own page (the Shared tabs): numbers, conversations, emails sent, calls and messages. Never money, costs,
the fit score, internal to-dos or other clients.
